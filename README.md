# Adidas US Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Adidas US sales performance.

The dashboard provides detailed analysis across regions, retailers, products, sales channels, and monthly sales trends to identify key business insights and performance patterns.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Adidas US sales data and understand:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Region-wise sales performance
- Retailer-wise contribution
- Product-wise sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Units sold and average selling price
- Profit margin performance
- Key areas contributing to business performance

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Region-wise Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across different US regions and helps identify regional contribution to overall sales and profit.

### 2. Retailer-wise Analysis

Retailer-level analysis is used to understand sales contribution across major Adidas retailers and sales channels.

### 3. Product-wise Analysis

Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing and low-performing product categories based on sales, units sold, profit, and margin.

### 4. Monthly Trend Analysis

Monthly analysis provides insights into changes in sales, units sold, profit, average selling price, and profit margin throughout the year.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Units Sold
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Average Selling Price (ASP)

## 🔍 Root Cause Analysis

The dashboard also supports deeper analysis by drilling down from:

**Region → State → Sales Channel → Product**

This helps identify the underlying factors contributing to sales and profit performance.

## 📊 Project Scope

The analysis focuses on Adidas US Sales data with detailed analysis for the year 2021.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Identified regional differences in Adidas sales performance.
- Compared retailer contribution to overall sales.
- Analyzed product categories based on sales and profitability.
- Identified monthly sales and profit trends.
- Used drill-down analysis to investigate business performance at a deeper level.
